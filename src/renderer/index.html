System Reset & Data Sanitization

Execute selective catalog maintenance or authorized database purge routines. Actions in this module permanently modify or wipe database records and cannot be undone.

Level 1 • Selective Catalog

Delete Single Medicine

Remove a discontinued medicine and its current batch stocks from the inventory catalog. Past sales orders and historical financial records are preserved.

Level 2 • High Risk Inventory

Clear All Medicines

Permanently wipe all registered drug items and active batch inventory stocks. Past sales orders, invoices, and analytics reports remain intact.

Level 3 • Financial Ledger

Clear Sales & Financials

Permanently wipe all sales transaction receipts, till shift balances, and revenue charts. Active medicine catalog and current stock levels are untouched.

Level 4 • Nuclear Wipe

Full Factory System Reset

Total wipe of all medicines, batch stock, sales invoices, till shifts, and audit logs. Restores the database to its pristine factory installation state.
